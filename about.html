About Habitap

Asia's first fully integrated smart living management platform.

Habitap seamlessly combines smart controls, community management and lifestyle offerings, three key features for modern day smart living, into a single platform. Harnessing the technology behind Internet of Things (IoT), Habitap aims to create better living, greater convenience and efficiency for commercial, residential and various spaces.

From commercial building spaces to homes and various spaces, Habitap consistently innovates to continue to improve the quality of life for residents and tenants everywhere.
Our focus

Three principles behind everything we build.

People

Habitap only builds solutions which are relevant and beneficial to people, improving their lives as the ultimate measure of our success.

Sustainability

We are focused on cutting waste in every aspect of our deployments. By reducing hardware through software. By reducing consumption of power in homes and offices through solution design and data. By emphasising sustainability in our product design.

Security

Habitap is the first of its kind to deploy banking grade security to protect your home or office. Using Banking Grade Security service Keypasco, we secure your user name and password to your personal mobile device. This ensures that only authenticated devices will be able to login to your home or office. As an organisation, we are ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27701 certified.

Certification and awards

Independently certified, independently recognised.

ISO/IEC 27001 & ISO/IEC 27701

ISO/IEC 27001 is widely known, providing requirements for an information security management system (ISMS), though there are more than a dozen standards in the ISO/IEC 27000 family. Using them enables organizations of any kind to manage the security of assets such as financial information, intellectual property, employee details or information entrusted by third parties. ISO/IEC 27701 extends that system with requirements for a privacy information management system (PIMS), covering the handling of personally identifiable information.
